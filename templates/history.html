Methodology

Observed markers show IV inverted from saved quotes using the existing European Black-Scholes-Merton pipeline. The fitted surface adjusts and interpolates those inputs. Spot changes and remaining maturity both affect the comparison. Internal repricing checks numerical consistency, not independent market accuracy or predictive performance.
